    Roadmap to the platinum

    Seven stages, about 200 hours, one playthrough. Story first, then the 100% checklist, then clean-up. Nothing on the checklist is missable.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides. Everything else — story trophies included — can be earned after the story in free roam.

        Time-sensitive, not missable

        Some stranger mission chains have windows tied to story progress. Take Andy, Kenton, Randy and Tyrone missions as they appear instead of leaving every strand for after the credits.
